## Thug

Thugs are people of rage and recklessness that abandon their own safety to better defeat their enemy.

**Thug Proficiencies**

- Combat Training: All Melee Weapons, All Shotgun Style Weapons, Light Armor, Light Shields, Medium Shields  
- Maneuvers: You can learn all Attack Maneuvers. You gain Maneuvers as shown in the Thug Class Table.  
- Focus Points: Your max number of Focus Points increases as shown in the Focus Points Column of the Thug Class Table.  
- Techniques: The number of Techniques you know increases as shown in the Techniques Known column of the Drifter Class Table.

**Starting Equipment**

- 3 Weapons you have Combat Training or Light Shields (each less than 100 dollars)  
- 1 Case of Ammo per Firearm you chose OR 20 Bolts/Arrows per Projectile weapon you chose  
- Padded Armor  
- Dungeoneer's OR Explorer's Pack

**Level 1**   
*Thuggish Focus*  
When you score a Heavy or Critical Hit against a creature OR if either is scored against you, you regain half of your FP, if you didn't spend FP as part of the action.

*Rage*   
During Combat, you can spend 1 AP and 1 FP to enter a Rage for 1 minute. For the duration, you're subjected to the following effects:

- You deal \+1 damage on Melee Attacks.  
- You have ADV on Brawn Checks and Saves.  
- Your AC decreases by 5\.  
- You gain Resistance(1/2) to Physical Damage. Your Rage ends early if you fall Unconscious, die, or you choose to end it for free on your turn.

*Unarmored Warrior*

- You gain access to the following AC formula when not wearing Armor: 8 \+ Combat Proficiency \+ (Reflex or Brawn) \+ 2  
- While not wearing Armor, you have \+1 Speed

*Aggressive Striker*

- Charge: When you make a Melee Attack on your turn, you can move up to 2 Spaces immediately before making the Attack.  
- Mighty Leap: You can use your Brawn instead of Reflex to determine your Jump Distance and the damage you take from Falling.

**Level 2**   
*Danger Sense*   
You have ADV on Initiative checks and when you are Surprised you are not Exposed.

*Reckless Attack*  
Once per turn, you can choose to Reckless Attack when you make a Melee Attack check. When you do so, that Attack has ADV, but you are Exposed against the next attack against you.

**Level 3**   
At level three you choose your subclass, known as Thuggish.

**Level 4**  
*Additional Feat* You gain access to 1 Feat of your choice

**Level 5**   
*Second Attack*  
When you make Attacks in combat you do not gain a stack of DIS until after your 2nd Attack per round.

*Angry Run*   
While Raging, your Speed is increased by 2 Spaces.

### Thuggish Reputation

**Berserker Reputation**  
Berserkers are simple but powerful, relying on their sheer force to defeat their enemies.

**Level 3:** *Unfathomable Strength*

- Titan Grip: While Raging, you can wield Two-Handed Weapons using only one hand.  
- Jump Good: Your Jump Distance increases by 1\.

*Swift Berserker*

- Quick to Anger: When you roll Initiative, you can spend 1 FP to Rage.  
- Unstoppable Charge: When you Charge (as granted by your *Aggressive Striker* ability), you ignore Difficult Terrain and do not provoke Attacks of Opportunity.

**Zealot Reputation**  
You faith allows you to push yourself beyond the abilities of normal people

**Level 3:**   
*Mantra*  
At the start of each day choose one of the following Mantras. When you do so, you gain the associated benefits:

- "We are one": While you're Raging, your allies have ADV on Melee Attack rolls against creatures within 1 Space of you.  
- "Forward with Faith": While Raging, increase your Speed by 2\.  
- "Guide My Hand": While Raging you have ADV when making Ranged Attacks with weapons with the Thrown property.  
- "May the Shadows Shield Me": If you attack a Surprised creature, you can choose to Rage as part of the attack. When you do so, add your Combat Proficiency to the damage.

*Mind of the Faithful*   
You gain Damage Reduction (x) against psychic damage, where x is equal to your Combat Proficiency
