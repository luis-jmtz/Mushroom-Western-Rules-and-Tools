<h1 style="text-align: center;">Feats</h1>

PCs gain Feats at specific levels to improve their statistics or gain new abilities.

If a Feat has a requirement (such as a Level requirement), you must meet that requirement to choose that Feat. You can only take a Feat once unless otherwise stated.

<h3 style="text-align: center;">Feats List</h3>

#### Attribute Increase
*General Feate, Can be taken more than once*
You gain 1 Attribute Point to put into any Attribute of your choice as long as it does not exceed your Attribute Limit

#### Additional Expertise 
*General Feat, can be taken more than once *
You gain Expertise with 1 skill of your choice.

#### Martial Expansion
*Martial Feat*   
You gain the following benefits:

- You learn 2 additional Maneuvers  
- You learn 1 additional Technique  
- You gain Combat Training with one of the following: Projectile Weapons, Firearms, Heavy Armor, Medium Shields, or Heavy Shields.

#### Skill Increase
*General Feat, can be taken more than once*
You gain 2 Skill points to put into any Skill or Trade of your choice unless it exceeds your Proficiency Level Limit.

#### Technique/Maneuver Expansion
*Martial Feat, can be taken more than once*
You learn 1 additional Technique or Manuever
